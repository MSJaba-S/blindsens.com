Description

Find your best mouse sensitivity by testing it blind. Play short aim blocks at five hidden sensitivities in random order. Blindsens fits a curve to how you actually perform and only recommends a change when the data is statistically confident.

What makes it different

  • It’s blind. Five hidden sensitivities, shuffled, so habit can’t bias the answer.
  • It’s a test, not a trainer. It answers which sens you aim best with, not how to practise more.
  • It’s honest. A curve through every block, 400 resamples and a curvature test. When the data can’t tell, it says so.
