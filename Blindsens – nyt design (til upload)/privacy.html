Privacy policy

The short versionThe Blindsens app stores everything on your own PC and sends nothing to us. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. If you email us, we use your email only to answer.

Who is responsible

The data controller for Blindsens and blindsens.com is Blindsens, M. Samir J., Dalslandsgade 10, 2300 Copenhagen S, Denmark, support@blindsens.com.

The Blindsens app

Your tests, results, profile and settings are stored locally on your computer. Blindsens has no user accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else. You can export, import or delete your data at any time under Settings.

Links in the app, for example to research sources, open in your own browser only when you click them.

Steam

Blindsens is sold and delivered through Steam. Valve processes your purchase, account and download under the Steam Privacy Policy. We only receive aggregated sales and usage reports from Valve, not your personal account details.

This website

blindsens.com uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. To keep the service secure, GitHub logs the IP address of visitors. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Email

If you write to support@blindsens.com or hello@blindsens.com, we use your email address and message only to answer you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Our mailbox is provided by Zoho Corporation. We delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed, at the latest two years after the last contact.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Write to support@blindsens.com. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

Changes

If Blindsens starts processing personal data in a new way, this page is updated before that change takes effect.
